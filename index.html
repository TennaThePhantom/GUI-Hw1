Welcome to the Golden Age of Fighting Games

The fighting game genre or as it's often called. The Fighting Game Community (FGC) has exploded with incredible releases, in the past few years and more coming soon. Tekken 8 arrived in 2024 with stunning Unreal Engine 5 graphics and the aggressive new Heat System. Street Fighter 6 (SF6) change the street fighter game series with the Drive Gauge system and Modern Controls, making it accessible to newcomers, while keeping deep competitive play. Mortal Kombat 1 (MK1) rebooted the iconic franchise with Kameo Fighters as a assist fighting game, fresh fatalities, and a unusual storyline. Guilty Gear Strive remains the gold standard for anime-like fighters with its incredible cinematic visuals and stunning gameplay. Dragon Ball FighterZ continues to dominate as the ultimate anime tag fighter, capturing the anime fans of the beloved series. Virtual Fighter 6(upcoming) promises to bring back realistic, meter-less 3D combat and A new way of fighting. Marvel Tokon(upcoming) The new Marvel fighting game, rumored to be a successor to Marvel vs. Capcom, is set to release in 2026. With 4v4 Battles instead of 3v3. Avatar Legends(upcoming) based on Avatar The Last Airbender from Nickelodeon in the 2000s is coming soon, featuring bending-based combat. This page covers all these amazing fighters. Whether you're a casual fan or a tournament competitor. There's never been a better time to jump into fighting games. Let's explore the modern titles the FGC has to offer
